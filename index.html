The last lamp is free.

Valli was the temple's lamp keeper and the village healer. She discovered that the headman had diverted the spring and forged the land records, leaving the lower hamlet to drown when the monsoon dam failed. She hid the copper deed and warning in the sanctum, then rang the bell to lead children to high ground. The doors were barred from outside. The village called her a thief to hide its shame.

Her blood was not a curse. It was the mark of a truth no one would hear. You have given her name back—and witnessed what was done.

At dawn, the lamp gutters out. The temple doors stand open. On the threshold, one anklet glints in the sun.
